Soyoung Park

Postdoctoral Researcher · InnoCORE · UNIST

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST), affiliated with the Safe AI Research Center under the InnoCORE program. I work with Prof. Yeon-Chang Lee in the Data Intelligence Lab.

My research focuses on trustworthy machine learning, particularly uncertainty quantification and fairness in graph neural networks. I also study LLM-guided recommendation, dynamic graphs for time-series modeling, and multimodal learning.

About

“Intuition, sharpened by analysis.”

I build research intuition from diverse experiences and refine it through rigorous analysis, evidence, and experimentation. I am particularly interested in problems where average performance alone is not enough.

Research Interests

  • Graph and structured learning: graph neural networks and recommendation.
