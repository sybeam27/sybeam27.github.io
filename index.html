Soyoung Park

Postdoctoral Researcher

I am a postdoctoral researcher interested in trustworthy and reliable machine learning. My research focuses on uncertainty quantification, fairness, and learning from graph-structured, temporal, and multimodal data.

Before focusing on academic research, I worked with real-world data across energy and public-sector projects. These experiences continue to shape the questions I ask and how I evaluate machine learning methods.
