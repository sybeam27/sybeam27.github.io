01 / ResearchQuestions that guide my work

Learning we
can rely on.

I work on reliable machine learning for structured, temporal, and multimodal data—with a particular interest in problems where average performance alone is not enough.

01

Uncertainty & Reliability

Beyond point estimates: understanding what a model knows, where it is uncertain, and when its predictions deserve trust.

02

Fairness & Heterogeneity

Looking beyond aggregate performance to understand how learning behaves across groups and differing data distributions.

03

Graph & Structured Learning

Learning from relationships and the structure of data, with interests in graph neural networks and recommendation.

04

Temporal & Multimodal Learning

Exploring how patterns evolve over time and how complementary modalities can inform more reliable learning.

04 / About

Different problems
teach different
ways of seeing.

For me, research begins with noticing something: a pattern, an unexpected result, or a gap between how a model performs and how it is used.

Diverse experiences shape that first intuition. Analysis and experiments help me challenge it, understand its limits, and turn it into a question worth pursuing.

I am particularly interested in uncertainty, fairness, distributional heterogeneity, and reliability—questions that ask us to look beyond a single performance number.
